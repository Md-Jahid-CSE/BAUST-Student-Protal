8. Academic Policies

What is the attendance rule for exams?

The rules are:

80% or more attendance: You are eligible for the final exam without any fine.

65% to 79.9% attendance: You are eligible, but you must pay a fine to get your admit card.

Less than 65% attendance: You are not eligible for the exam.

What if my attendance is below 65% for a valid reason?

In an emergency (like severe illness), you can apply to the Head of the Department for special permission. Your application must include valid proof (e.g., a doctor's certificate) and be recommended by your Course Advisor. The Head's decision is final.

What is a RIB exam?

RIB stands for Referred/Improvement/Backlog exam. It's a special exam held after the semester final.

Referred/Backlog: For students who failed a course.

Improvement: For students who passed but want to improve their grade.

What happens if I fail a course in both the Final and RIB exams?

If you fail a course in the final exam and then fail it again in the RIB exam, your final result for that course will be an 'F' grade. This will lower your GPA/CGPA, and you will have to retake the entire course as a backlog with a junior batch in the future.

Does failing one or two courses mean I get a semester drop?

No. Failing a few courses means you have to retake them (backlog). A semester drop is more severe and usually happens if your overall CGPA falls below the university's minimum requirement or if you fail a very large number of courses, resulting in a "Failed" status on your final marksheet.

What's the difference between a "Failed" and "Referred" status on the marksheet?

"Failed" status: This is very serious. It usually means you have failed 3-5 courses (or more, depending on university rules) and results in an immediate Semester Drop. You must repeat the entire semester with a junior batch. There is no second chance.

"Referred" status: This is a final chance to avoid a semester drop. It means you must sit for the RIB exam for the courses you failed. If you pass the RIB exam, you clear the semester. If you fail the RIB exam, you will then get a semester drop.
