9. ACADEMIC POLICIES
================================================================================

ATTENDANCE RULES
----------------
- ≥80%: Eligible for final exam without fine
- 65-79.9%: Eligible but must pay fine for admit card
- <65%: Not eligible (unless special permission)

SPECIAL PERMISSION
------------------
- Apply to Head of Department with valid proof
- Requires Course Advisor recommendation
- Head's decision is final

RIB EXAMS
---------
RIB = Referred/Improvement/Backlog exam after final results
- Referred/Backlog: For students who failed
- Improvement: For students wanting better grades

FAILURE CONSEQUENCES
--------------------
- Fail final + RIB → Final grade 'F' → Retake as backlog with junior batch

"FAILED" VS "REFERRED"
----------------------
- "Failed": May cause semester drop in severe cases
- "Referred": Second chance via RIB exam
